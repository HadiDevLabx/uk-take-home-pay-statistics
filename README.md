# UK Take-Home Pay Statistics 2026/27 — Free to Cite

> Take-home pay, effective rates and marginal rates from £20,000 to £200,000, computed from HMRC rates and free to cite with attribution.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

HMRC publishes gross income distributions. Almost nobody publishes the **net** figure beside them, which is what this set exists for.

| Gross | Take-home | Effective rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| £20,000 | £17,919.60 | 10.4% |
| £50,000 | £39,519.60 | 21.0% |
| £100,000 | £68,557.40 | 31.4% |
| £200,000 | £117,786.40 | 41.1% |

The rate tables are also published as machine-readable JSON at [rates.json](https://truetakehome.co.uk/rates.json), described by an [OpenAPI specification](https://truetakehome.co.uk/openapi.json). No key, no rate limit.

## What this does not cover

Figures assume a standard tax code and no pension or student loan, so they describe the tax system rather than any individual's payslip.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
